# Kelvor — Company Overview

Kelvor is an independent digital product studio based in India, working globally.

Positioning (verbatim from kelvor.co.in):
- "We build digital products worth remembering."
- "Strategy, design and engineering — from first idea to shipped product."
- "A small team with a big focus."
- Footer: "Independent product studio building thoughtful digital products."

## What we do (four service pillars)
1. Product Strategy & Design — "From early ideas to clear product direction, intuitive UX, and polished interfaces."
2. Web & Creative Development — "High-performance websites and digital experiences built with motion, interaction, and attention to detail."
3. Full-Stack Engineering — "Reliable applications and scalable systems engineered from frontend to backend."
4. MVP Build & Launch — "From concept to a working product — designed, engineered, tested, and ready to ship."

## How we work (five-step process)
1. Discover — Understand the problem, users, and opportunity.
2. Define — Turn insights into a clear product direction.
3. Design — Shape the experience, interface, and interactions.
4. Build — Engineer the product with production-ready systems.
5. Launch — Ship, refine, and prepare for what comes next.
