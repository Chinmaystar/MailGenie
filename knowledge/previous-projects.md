Brand, storefront, accounts, custom photo-upload → WhatsApp design-approval (6–8h) → print-after-confirmation → 4–6 working day delivery pipeline, ₹49-advance payment model, and WhatsApp/Instagram operations. Full details in case-studies.md and portfolio.md.
- **Kelvor studio site (kelvor.co.in)** — motion- and interaction-heavy creative site presenting the studio's services, process, and team, with a project-inquiry form.
- **CafeMitra / Cafe ERP** — multi-tenant café management platform: QR public table ordering, POS, kitchen display, menu + bulk CSV import, inventory, staff management, live revenue and average-order-value analytics.
- **Medio** — map-based meetup planner on Vercel: Leaflet map with participant origin/destination markers, computed meeting points with venue selection, date-time scheduling, Google sign-in.
