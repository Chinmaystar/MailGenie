## Totemood — custom illustrated tote bags (totemood.com)

Kelvor's own live product: an e-commerce brand where customers turn a personal photo into a custom illustrated tote bag. Kelvor built the brand, design experience, and full storefront end to end.

Positioning (verbatim):
- "From your photo to a Timeless Tote"
- "Premium Illustrated Totes"
- "Bags that speak." / "Every memory deserves to be carried."

What it demonstrates about Kelvor's capabilities:
- Full custom e-commerce build: product catalog with collections, custom totes, and bestsellers; customer accounts and sign-in; reviews; content sections (Stories, About, FAQ).
- A custom order workflow most storefronts don't have: customer uploads a photo with their order, the design is delivered to WhatsApp for approval within 6–8 hours, minor changes can be requested, and printing starts only after the customer confirms the final design. Delivery within 4–6 working days of approval.
- A custom payment flow for made-to-order goods: ₹49 advance at checkout to confirm the order (deducted from the final amount), balance paid on delivery — instead of full cash-on-delivery, which doesn't work for custom products.
- Policies engineered around personalization: customized products are non-returnable; returns/replacements only for damaged, defective, or wrong items with unboxing-video proof.
- Operations built around WhatsApp (wa.me deep links) and Instagram (@totemood_gifts). Based in Mumbai, India.

Use Totemood as the go-to proof point in outreach: it is a shipped, live product showing Kelvor can take an idea from brand concept to a working custom-commerce business.

## CafeMitra (Cafe ERP) — café management platform

A multi-tenant café operations platform Kelvor built (accessed via a private deployment URL). Evidence from the live app:

- Multi-cafe management: owners can create multiple cafes with owner credentials, "Cafe Owner Login" and "Cafe Owner Panel", "Cafes Covered" metrics.
- QR-style table ordering: each table gets its own "Final Public Table URL" (copyable cafe URL), customers browse the menu and place orders per table, and "each table order keeps its own timeline" with "Finish Table" / "Finalize Order" flows.
- POS and kitchen: order status updates in the POS, a kitchen display ("Kitchen is working"), order history.
- Menu management: menu item CRUD plus "Bulk Upload Menu CSV" / "Import CSV to Cafe" for onboarding a catalog quickly.
- Inventory management: item-level inventory with CSV download/import.
- Analytics: dashboard with live order revenue from today, average order value, cafes covered, and other KPIs.
- Staff management: add/edit staff per cafe.

Use CafeMitra as the proof point when a prospect needs restaurant/café software, POS, QR ordering, multi-tenant SaaS dashboards, or operations tooling.

## BoostAI — AI edtech platform (in active development)

An AI-based edtech platform Kelvor is building. The core capability: AI models generate practice questions similar to a provided one, so students can drill unlimited fresh variations of the same problem with step-by-step AI explanations. The planned product (concept and landing source at boostai.study) defines two routes:

- School mode — "Ace GCSE, A-Level, IGCSE, and IB questions with step-by-step AI explanations and unlimited practice variations."
- University mode — "Turn dense lectures, readings, and past papers into revision prompts, worked answers, and next-step study plans."

Status: real, active Kelvor project with a concept build (boostai.study); the full product is still in development — no live deployment yet.
