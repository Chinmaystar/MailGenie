# Kelvor — Services

Kelvor offers four service pillars (verbatim names from kelvor.co.in). Everything below describes what Kelvor does for clients — capabilities, not past-project claims.

## Product Strategy & Design
"From early ideas to clear product direction, intuitive UX, and polished interfaces."
- Product discovery and direction for early-stage ideas
- UX design and interface design
- Design systems and polished, production-ready UI

## Web & Creative Development
"High-performance websites and digital experiences built with motion, interaction, and attention to detail."
- Marketing and brand sites with motion, interaction, and scroll experiences
- Interactive, map- and geolocation-driven web apps — live proof: Medio, a map-based meetup planner
- High-performance, detail-obsessed web experiences
- Live example: kelvor.co.in itself

## Full-Stack Engineering
"Reliable applications and scalable systems engineered from frontend to backend."
- Custom web applications (React/Next.js/Node/TypeScript)
- E-commerce platforms with custom admin panels and CMS
- Multi-tenant SaaS with role-based access — live proof: CafeMitra (café ERP with QR table ordering, POS, kitchen display, inventory, analytics)
- AI/LLM product features — live proof: BoostAI, an AI edtech platform Kelvor is building that generates practice questions similar to a provided one
- Payment integrations (Razorpay, Stripe, PayPal) and custom payment flows — including made-to-order models like advance-plus-balance-on-delivery
- Shipping/logistics integrations (Shiprocket, Delhivery) and custom order workflows
- Internal tools, admin dashboards, RBAC, reporting
- API development: REST/GraphQL, third-party integrations, webhooks, auth
- DevOps: Vercel/AWS/GCP/Railway deployments, CI/CD, Docker, database design

## MVP Build & Launch
"From concept to a working product — designed, engineered, tested, and ready to ship."
- End-to-end MVP delivery: strategy, design, engineering, testing, launch
- Live example: Totemood (totemood.com) — a custom-photo tote e-commerce business Kelvor built and operates, including a bespoke photo-upload → WhatsApp design-approval → print-after-confirmation pipeline

## How engagements run
Five-step process: Discover → Define → Design → Build → Launch. Small team of four builders; clients work directly with the builders. Project-based, scoped per engagement via the inquiry form on kelvor.co.in.
